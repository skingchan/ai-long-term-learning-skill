# Assessment, mastery, and evidence protocol

## Mastery scale

| Level | Label | Required observable behavior |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Unknown | No usable evidence, or not yet exposed |
| 1 | Recognize | Identifies concept/example, cannot reliably explain or use it |
| 2 | Assisted | Completes with worked example, hints, checklist or tutor guidance |
| 3 | Independent | Correctly completes a representative new task without scaffolding and explains why |
| 4 | Transfer/Judgment | Applies in novel context, weighs trade-offs, finds errors, can teach or justify |

These are **proficiency levels**, not exam percentages. Evidence always includes task, date, independence conditions, strengths, limitations and confidence. Avoid assigning 4 merely for confident speech.

## Lesson acceptance

Define the pass criterion **before** asking. Use at least one uncoached, changed problem; include a why/when explanation. For low-stakes factual mini-quizzes, 80–90% may be an indicative threshold; critical conceptual failures override averages. If voice, playback, application, execution or other evidence cannot be inspected, mark the corresponding skill `unverified`.

## Module acceptance

Require combining multiple lessons, a delayed recall or independent challenge, and one output in the medium the discipline actually uses. Evaluate process and result. A borrowed/AI-authored artifact by itself establishes collaboration evidence, not learner proficiency.

## Stage Gate

1. Re-state the stage's competency list and pre-registered success criteria.
2. Collect artifacts and record which were independently produced.
3. Run an unseen synthesis task under realistic constraints.
4. Use a rubric covering correctness, reasoning, independence, transfer and discipline-specific safety/quality.
5. Give one of: `PASS`, `CONDITIONAL_PASS`, `REPAIR_AND_RETEST`, `CHALLENGE_OR_ACCELERATE`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
6. Export evidence ledger, repairs, next checkpoint and snapshot delta.

## Feedback format

- **Observed submission:** quote or reference actual output.
- **Correct:** exact components and why.
- **Incorrect/missing:** location, consequence, likely cause; distinguish concept, strategy, fluency, attention, environment.
- **Learner repair:** one concrete action.
- **Fresh retest:** new, not trivial paraphrase.
- **Decision and evidence:** pass/pending; no fictional grade.
- **Record update:** `skill_id | level | date | evidence | confidence | revisit`.

## Authenticity rules

- A published app does not mean the learner designed or understood its architecture.
- A correct response to a revealed solution is not an independent test.
- A mock negotiation is not proof of real-world transaction skill.
- A profitable week is not proof of trading edge.
- An outline of a game is not a working game.
- A paragraph about playing piano is not audio performance.
- A quiz score from incomplete answers cannot be extrapolated.

If unavailable artifacts are necessary, describe the assessment limitation rather than claiming to have reviewed them.
