# Example: Systematic mathematics training

## Position
A long-term mathematics learning direction was discussed; the exact historical syllabus, target level and baseline are **not verified in this example**. Ask the learner to confirm whether the goal is general foundations, university mathematics, applied mathematics or a particular specialty. This template is a **provisional** pathway, not a claimed recovered schedule.

## Provisional stages
1. Number sense, algebraic operations, equations, inequalities, functions.
2. Geometry, coordinate reasoning, trigonometry and modeling.
3. Mathematical proof, sets, logic, induction and combinatorics.
4. Calculus: limits, derivatives, integrals, applications.
5. Linear algebra, probability/statistics, discrete mathematics.
6. Selected advanced/applied modules based on target: optimization, differential equations, analysis, algorithms, modeling.
7. Independent problem-solving portfolio and cross-topic synthesis.

## Diagnosis
Ask for one simple symbolic manipulation, one explanatory "why" question, one word problem needing a model, and one proof or counterexample. Adapt to verified proficiency.

## Gate examples
- Solve a genuinely new family of problems, not an identical worked exercise.
- Show derivation and conditions under which the method works.
- Diagnose a wrong proof or numerical argument, then build a correct alternative.

**No preset mastery assumption.** Replace this provisional map after real diagnostic results.
