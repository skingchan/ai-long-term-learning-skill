# Example: Long-term English training

## Position
English was among the previously planned training directions. The precise learner baseline, goals, and confirmed stage order are **not carried into this public example**. Confirm intended uses (reading, speaking, work, travel, content creation) and preferred accent/register before locking the course.

## Provisional stages
1. Adaptive diagnostic across listening, speaking, reading, writing, vocabulary and grammar.
2. Foundational pronunciation/phonology, high-frequency phrases and sentence building.
3. Practical comprehension: short audio, conversations and graded texts.
4. Conversation: turn taking, questions, clarification, spontaneous response.
5. Writing: accurate everyday messages → structured explanations → edited essays/work artifacts.
6. Domain-specific English with authentic material and interaction.
7. Sustained independent communication assessed across all four modalities.

## Evidence & limitations
Sample real listening comprehension; live oral speech or recordings where supported; unscripted explanation; independent new writing with revisions. Text-only chat cannot certify pronunciation or audio listening performance; mark those skills unknown or use user-provided recordings with available tools.

## First diagnostic
Use a short graded reading/comprehension task, independent 100-word writing sample, and brief unscripted self-introduction. Add listening/speaking when media access exists.

**Do not infer fluency from grammar worksheets alone.**
