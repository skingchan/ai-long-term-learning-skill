# Example: Traffic × demand × supply × monetization

## Known goal and background
The long-term goal is a repeatable ability to discover online needs, anomalies, pain points, information asymmetries, scarcity and platform changes; understand traffic sources and paths; build or broker useful supply; test conversion and profit. Digital-nomad-like internet livelihood capabilities matter. The learner has prior **Shopify and Facebook/Meta Ads practical experience**, so this should not be taught as if they have never run a shop or campaign.

## Proposed stages
1. Initial diagnosis: map prior acquisition campaigns, conversion funnels, product work and evidence of profitability.
2. Demand discovery: search intent, communities, interviews, behavior, high-friction workflows, competitor complaints, anomaly detection.
3. Offer/value: service vs content vs tool vs marketplace vs physical product; information advantage, differentiation, economics, positioning.
4. Traffic: SEO, social, communities, paid distribution, partnership, discovery mechanisms; measure channels without confusing impressions with buying intent.
5. Conversion: message-market fit, landing pages, UX, trust, analytics, experiments.
6. Supply and fulfillment: AI-produced tools/content, no-code/code, suppliers, operations, quality and compliance.
7. Unit economics and repeatability: CAC, gross contribution, refunds, retention, validation thresholds, iteration and stop rules.
8. Capstone: launch a small verifiable product/offer, document real behavior and decision rationale.

## Example first diagnostic
Ask for a brief postmortem of a *real past* funnel: source → click → page → purchase / lead → costs → repeat. Do not require private account access; numerical ranges and anonymized evidence suffice.

## Gates
- 10 independently assessed opportunities with hypotheses and cited observations (no fabricated demand).
- A measured test plan with falsifiable criteria, budget/time limits and defined outcome.
- A real demand-to-supply-to-conversion experiment; report uncertainty, expenses and failures.

**Never** count speculative idea scores or X posts as validated revenue.
